# Privacy Policy for FitFlow Mobile Application
**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Version:** 2.0.0  
**Hosted URL:** https://yomalisenevirathne-2003.github.io/fitflow-redesign/privacy-policy.html

## 1. Introduction
FitFlow ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding user privacy. This policy outlines how we handle personal data, on-device AI inference, and social interactions under GDPR, CCPA, and health data regulations.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Profile Information:** Name, email, age, gender, and base fitness level.
- **Fitness & Sensor Data:** Workouts logged, duration, sets/reps, and activity trends.
- **Nutrition Data & Images:** Photos captured via camera for nutritional analysis.
- **Technical Diagnostics:** Anonymized crash logs and device models via Firebase Crashlytics.

## 3. AI Processing & Computer Vision
- **On-Device Machine Learning:** Wherever possible, workout recommendations and camera vision models run on-device using TensorFlow Lite to minimize cloud transmission.
- **Cloud Processing:** When deeper meal analysis is needed, images are processed over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections and discarded once macro estimations are completed.
- **No Data Selling:** We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade your biometrics or health metrics to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Social Features & Private Circles
- FitFlow features private accountability circles. By default, workouts are private unless explicitly shared to a user's selected circle.
- Users can modify or revoke social visibility settings at any time.

## 5. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
Users maintain full ownership of their data:
- **Right to Access & Portability:** Request an export of your full health and nutrition logs.
- **Right to Rectification:** Update inaccurate personal records.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Delete your entire account and associated records immediately from Settings.
