# FitFlow Release Notes - Version 2.0.0 (Build 200)

### 🌟 What's New in FitFlow 2.0:
- **AI Daily Flow:** Instant personalized workout recommendations tailored to your schedule and recovery level.
- **Snap & Log Nutrition:** Camera-based food recognition that identifies meals in seconds—no more tedious manual typing.
- **Private Accountability Circles:** Connect, share milestones, and participate in challenges with friends in a safe, controlled space.
- **Celebratory Milestones:** New achievement badges, streak animations, and motivating post-workout summaries.
- **Visual Progress Dashboard:** Redesigned clean charts showing weekly volume, consistency, and fitness trends at a glance.

### 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Usability Enhancements:
- Streamlined the onboarding questionnaire to prevent drop-off.
- Fixed an issue where workout logs failed during poor network connectivity by introducing local offline persistence.
- Improved graph contrast and typography for better accessibility (WCAG AA compliant).
